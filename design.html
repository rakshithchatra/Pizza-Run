Pizza Run

Design sheet. Everything below is the real in-game art, rendered live.

128 02,450 2x II

Look

Chunky low-poly city at golden hour. Warm, edible colours; nothing photoreal.

How it plays

  • Three lanes. Swipe left / right to switch, up to jump, down to slide.
  • Slices are worth 1 pizza point, a whole pie is worth 10.
  • Speed ramps up the longer you survive.
  • Pizza points bank into a wallet that carries between runs.

Runners

Three to start. Same size and moves, different outfits.

Pizza points

They spin and bob in the lane.

Obstacles

Each one reads at a glance: go around, jump, or slide.

City kit

Streets are assembled at random from these pieces, so no two runs look the same.
